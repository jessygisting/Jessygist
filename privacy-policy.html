Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Welcome to JessyGist. We publish news, entertainment, sports, celebrity stories, and trending content. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website.

If you contact us or submit a story, you may provide information such as your name, email address, and the content of your message. We use this information to review submissions and respond to enquiries. Please do not submit passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information.

Our hosting provider and any analytics or advertising services we use may collect technical information, such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and usage data. The information collected depends on the services enabled on the website.

Cookies and Advertising

JessyGist may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functions, understand visitor activity, or display advertisements. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to their applicable policies and user settings.

Visitors can manage cookies through their browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features or advertising functionality.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve the website, respond to messages, review submitted stories, protect the site against abuse, understand audience activity, and support advertising where enabled.

Third-Party Services

Links to other websites and services may appear on JessyGist. Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review the policies of services they use.

Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above or as required by applicable law. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of online storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's Privacy

JessyGist is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in ways prohibited by applicable law. A parent or guardian may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information that should be reviewed or removed.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You may contact us using the details provided on our Contact Us page to make a request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
